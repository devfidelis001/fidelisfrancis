/about

I’m Fidelis, a Nigerian developer and aspiring entrepreneur. I’m interested in how technology can turn ideas into products, businesses, and solutions that reach people at scale.

I work across software development, from designing interfaces and building Django backends to deploying applications to the cloud. Through this work, I’m learning how engineering, innovation, and business strategy come together to build sustainable companies.

Inspired by Elon Musk’s approach to engineering, ambitious thinking, and building at scale, I aim to develop the technical and entrepreneurial skills needed to create impactful technology businesses.

My focus is on building practical software, understanding real-world problems, and turning solutions into sustainable ventures that create value.

As the founder and builder behind Your Labour Market, I’m taking steps toward a bigger ambition: building technology-driven businesses that contribute to Africa’s technological and economic development.
